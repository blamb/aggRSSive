Try it without an account

An anonymous account has no email and no password. It has a secret link instead: keep the link and you keep the account, with everything you build in it. Lose the link and the account is gone.

Nothing about you is stored beyond the link itself; the privacy page lists what the site keeps. It works like any account here: add feeds, tag them, build and publish aggRSSives. You can turn it into a regular account later, from the Account page, and keep everything.
